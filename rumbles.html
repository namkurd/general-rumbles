Actual

Shows the most recently completed week's real standings: Rumbles, Rumble %, PF, PA, H2H, and Vs. Field are all locked in from that finished week. Once a new week kicks off, it tracks the actual points scored so far and shows what this week's Rumbles would be if the week ended right now, without adding them to the season Rumbles total yet. Hover or tap a team's Pts This Week to see exactly which players have contributed to that score.

Projected

The tab to watch during a live week. It scores the week using projected points and folds the result straight into the season totals, giving an early look at where the standings could land if the week ended right now. Hover or tap a team's Pts This Week to see which players are still to play or currently in a game, along with their kickoff time.

In both tabs, manager names are colored by this week's matchup, and whichever manager is currently ahead has their This Week and Pts This Week values colored to match.

Standings refresh automatically every 30 seconds during a live week, or hit Refresh now for an update right away.
